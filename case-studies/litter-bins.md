# Frankfort Avenue litter bins

**What happened:** through the Center for Neighborhoods' Neighborhood Institute (fall 2017), I proposed litter cans at TARC stops on Frankfort Avenue, won a Center for Neighborhoods micro grant for the class project, and took it to Metro Council. Council Member Bill Hollander (District 9) funded it. Eighteen cans were in place by March 2018.

## The public record

District 9's eNews of March 29, 2018 reports "Eighteen new trash receptacles" placed at bus stops along Frankfort Avenue, paid for with District 9 Capital Infrastructure Funds. [Bulletin](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/KYLOUISVILLE/bulletins/1e59595)

The bulletin confirms the outcome, count, place, date and funding. My part comes from my own account and my 2018 emails.

## What I did

- Wrote the proposal and the grant application.
- Hand canvassed the stops and made a zine-style proposal book.
- Took the proposal to Council members myself.
- After the District 9 bins went in, a second council member reached out about doing the same in his district.

## What it shows

A resident can write a proposal, win a small grant, work the council process directly and see results in months. That is the community side of the table this role works with.
