# BROKEN SIDEWALK

**What it is:** an exhibition proposal I submitted on January 3, 2022 to Louisville Visual Art's CPI public art program. **One of three finalists.** It was not selected in the final round.

## THE CONCEPT

- An outdoor installation at TARC stops across Louisville, printed like ads on the sides of the stops so riders see it while they wait.
- One side: curated literary and visual work by local artists and writers.
- The other side: AI-assisted art, to ask what role AI can play in urban design and public art. This was 2022, before most people were asking.
- Opening: a pop-up in an alley, plus a small zine listing the stops with pictures and stories.
- Theme: the sidewalk as the great equalizer, and how small bits of art in shared public space change how neighbors see each other.
- Proposed partners: TARC and the Center for Neighborhoods. I talked to TARC about ad space while building the proposal.

## WHAT IT SHOWS

Community, public space and careful thinking about AI in a community, years before this job existed.
