# WEBSITE ACCESSIBILITY AUDITS

**What it is:** a self-started accessibility review of the Jefferson County PVA website, done twice: a WCAG 2.2 AA audit in April 2026, and a WCAG 2.1 AA re-audit checklist in November 2026 for the federal deadline.

## APRIL 2026: WCAG 2.2 AA AUDIT

- Reviewed the homepage, disability statement and linked pages for barriers to people with disabilities.
- Delivered a formatted report with **11 findings**, a plain-language explanation of each, a recommended fix and a priority (immediate, high, medium, ongoing).
- Noted what the site already does well: descriptive page titles, consistent navigation, breadcrumbs, several ways to contact the office and a disability statement page.
- Examples of findings: no "skip to main content" link, a chat link that does not work for keyboard users, search boxes labeled only by placeholder text, support videos without captions, and a mismatch between an award badge's text and its image.
- Included a testing tools list (WAVE, Axe, a contrast checker, screen readers) and a note that the audit is advisory and a formal audit by a certified auditor is still recommended.

## NOVEMBER 2026: RE-AUDIT FOR THE 2027 DEADLINE

- Researched the rule that applies: federal ADA Title II web accessibility requires **WCAG 2.1 AA by April 26, 2027** for entities serving 50,000 or more people. I caught the change to the federal deadline before the official notice.
- Built a checklist covering all **50 WCAG 2.1 A and AA success criteria**, with a first-pass result for each, plus an inventory of 68 pages, videos, portals, maps and documents on the site.
- Compared the new review against April's 11 findings after the website vendor made changes: 2 were not fixed, 2 were partly fixed, 2 no longer applied under the older standard and 5 needed hands-on testing.
- Re-audit is ongoing.

## WHAT IT SHOWS

Reading a new rule early, turning it into a checklist other people can work from, and following up to see what really changed.

I do not publish the full findings list for a live government website. I am glad to walk through the method.
